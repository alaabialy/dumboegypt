    About

    Big flavor, no fuss.

    Dumbo is a Cairo fast food spot with a simple idea: cook great food and serve it fast. Juicy shawerma, grilled kofta and shrimp, made fresh every day.

    Made fresh

    Shawerma, kofta and shrimp, cooked fresh in Cairo.

    Big on flavor

    Simple ideas done well: juicy, grilled and full of taste.

    Served fast

    Dine in, call or order delivery. Your bite arrives quickly.

    What we cook

    • ShawermaMeat or chicken, in sandwiches, rolls and boxes.
    • Kofta & HawawshiGrilled kofta in a tortilla roll, and hawawshi plain, spicy or with cheese.
    • ShrimpShrimp sandwiches and shrimp packs with fries or bread.
    • ChickenChicken Blazing and Ninja Strips.
    • CrinkleCrinkle fries, plain, cheesy or topped with strips and shawerma.
